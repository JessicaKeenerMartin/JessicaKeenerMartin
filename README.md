# Hi, I'm Jessica Martin

### Behavioral Health Operations • Admissions • Program Management

I’m an experienced behavioral health professional based in Western North Carolina. My background spans admissions, program operations, supportive housing, staff leadership, compliance, client coordination, and day-to-day problem solving in fast-moving treatment environments.

I do my best work where organization and compassion have to exist at the same time: helping people access care, keeping teams aligned, and building practical systems that make programs run better.

- Program and facility operations
- Bed management and client placement
- Staff supervision, scheduling, hiring, and onboarding
- Compliance preparation, audits, and documentation
- Vendor and contractor coordination
- Supportive-housing and transportation logistics
- Crisis response and continuity planning
- Clear communication with clients, families, clinicians, and leadership

## Selected experience

I progressed from direct client support into admissions and program leadership, eventually overseeing operations across treatment programming and multiple supportive-living residences. My work has included coordinating interdisciplinary schedules, supervising staff, maintaining readiness for regulatory review, managing facilities and vendors, supporting intake decisions, and helping services continue through emergencies.

A defining experience in my career was helping maintain client care during Hurricane Helene despite extended power and water outages. That experience reinforced my belief that strong operations are ultimately about people: preparation matters because people depend on the systems we build.
